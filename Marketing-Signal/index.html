Learn marketing management, lesson by lesson

Marketing Signal is a free, open course that covers a full marketing management curriculum in 23 original lessons. It starts with what marketing actually does and how to write a strategy you can run, then moves through research and forecasting, how consumers and organizations buy, segmentation, positioning and brand equity. Later modules cover product and service strategy, launching new offerings, pricing, channels and retail logistics, and every major form of communication from advertising and events to search, social and personal selling. The course ends with how to build a marketing team that lasts. Each module lists clear learning objectives, an estimated read time and practical examples, and the glossary and search help you find any term fast. It is free to read, with no account needed.
