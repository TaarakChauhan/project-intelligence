What Founder Path is

Original Project Intelligence curriculum · September 2026

Founder Path is an original educational curriculum inside Project Intelligence. It is written for founders, owner-operators, and early leaders who need practical judgment under pressure. The lessons cover commitment, resilience under criticism, sales as core work, hiring and culture, leadership habits, operating rhythm, courage, and purpose.

All copy here is original to Project Intelligence. This curriculum is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any third-party book, publisher, or training brand. Theme areas reflect common founder challenges. The wording, frameworks, examples, and checklists are our own.

Who it is for

  • People deciding whether to start or continue a venture
  • Operators who sell, hire, and set weekly rhythm themselves
  • Leaders who want calm, specific practice rather than slogans

How to use it

  1. Begin at the curriculum index and read lessons in order once.
  2. Complete each practice checklist before moving on.
  3. Return to a single lesson when that pressure shows up at work.
  4. Keep notes in your own language so the ideas become operating habits.
Best progress comes from short, frequent application with real customers, real candidates, and real decisions, not from collecting more theory.
