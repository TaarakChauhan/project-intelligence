Survival of the fittest lesson pack

This is the printable teaching pack. For the short online course, go to What survival of the fittest really means.

A short educational pack for a curious learner. Read in order. The files open as markdown in the browser or in any text editor.

The folk meaning of the phrase is “the strongest win.” The scientific meaning is natural selection: heritable differences that affect reproductive success change populations over time. Fitness is not strength, virtue, or rank on a ladder.

  1. Begin START_HERE.md Welcome, how to use the pack, one-page overview
  2. Study plan 00-learning-plan.md Five sessions, 20–30 minutes each, in order
  3. Lesson 1 01-what-it-means.md What the phrase actually means; Spencer, Darwin, fitness
  4. Lesson 2 02-how-selection-works.md Variation, heredity, differential reproduction; moths, resistance, finches
  5. Lesson 3 03-misconceptions.md Ladders, tooth-and-claw, cooperation, “natural = good,” social Darwinism
  6. Lesson 4 04-seeing-it-today.md Medicine, conservation, agriculture, human evolution myths vs evidence
  7. Reference 05-glossary.md One-sentence definitions plus “not the same as”
  8. Check quiz.md Ten questions; answer key at the bottom
  9. Apply worksheet.md Fill-in prompts on a new example, not just recall
