Practice plan

30-day public speaking plan

~30–45 minutes most days · Longer on speech days

Goal: build confidence, clarity, and a repeatable process. Tools: phone camera, timer, notebook. Optional: free Coursera/Saylor enrollment, Toastmasters guest visit.

Week 1 — Foundations & nerves

Day 1
Mindset. Watch Joe Kowan (stage fright) + Chris Anderson (throughline). Write why you want to speak better in 3 sentences.
Day 2
Throughline. Pick a 2-minute topic. Write one throughline sentence. Cut anything that doesn’t support it.
Day 3
Structure. Outline Hook → 3 points → Close. Deliver once to camera.
Day 4
Vocal warm-up. Watch Julian Treasure. Do vocal exercises. Re-record Day 3 with more variety.
Day 5
PREP drill. Answer 5 random questions with PREP (60 sec each). Record.
Day 6
Feedback. Watch recordings. Note fillers/min, pace, eye contact, clarity.
Day 7
Rest / review. Read a chapter of Anderson or Gallo, or skim Six Minutes’ “7 Deadly Sins.”

Checkpoint: throughline habit + baseline video.

Week 2 — Craft & openings

Day 8
Openings. Write 3 openings (story / question / startling fact). Film all three.
Day 9
Story. Add one STAR story under 90 seconds.
Day 10
Sticky ideas. Apply SUCCESs to make one point more concrete + unexpected.
Day 11
Body language. Open posture; deliberate gestures. Film waist-up.
Day 12
Informative talk. Prep 4–5 min talk (outline only, no full script).
Day 13
Deliver. Film the informative talk. No restarting mid-take.
Day 14
Evaluate. Score 1–5 on structure, opening, vocal variety, gestures, close.

Checkpoint: stronger openings + one complete informative talk on video.

Week 3 — Persuasion & live practice

Day 15
Monroe. Outline a persuasive talk with all 5 steps.
Day 16
Duarte contrast. Mark “what is” vs “what could be.”
Day 17
Deliver persuasion. Film 5-min talk ending with clear Action.
Day 18
Q&A. Friend asks 5 tough questions; answer with PREP.
Day 19
Toastmasters / peer. Visit a club as guest or exchange video feedback.
Day 20
Impromptu. 10× 60-second random-topic talks.
Day 21
Review. Compare Week 1 vs Week 3 videos. List 3 wins + 1 focus.

Checkpoint: persuasive structure + at least one live/peer feedback loop.

Week 4 — Polish & systems

Day 22
Signature talk. Build your 5–7 min signature talk with throughline + outline.
Day 23
Rehearse. Aloud 3× standing. Time it. Cut to time.
Day 24
Full dress. Film once as if live. Optional Yoodli/Orai for filler stats.
Day 25
Checklist. Fix top 2 delivery issues; re-film.
Day 26
Nerves routine. 4-7-8 ×3, open stance 60s, first line memorized.
Day 27
Live or simulated. Deliver to a small real audience.
Day 28
Q&A mastery. Take 5 minutes of questions; record answers.
Day 29
Reflect. What works? What’s hard? Next 30-day focus?
Day 30
Systematize. Schedule 1 filmed practice/week + club/peer feedback 2×/month.

Daily micro-drills (any day, 5 minutes)

  1. Random word: speak 60 seconds on a random object.
  2. One breath pause: insert a full silent pause at each period.
  3. First line: deliver your opening line 5 times with energy.
  4. Filler hunt: 60-second talk; count um/uh; redo aiming for half.

Pair with lessons in Learn and links in Resources.
