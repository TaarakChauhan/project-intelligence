# Quick-Start 30-Day Public Speaking Plan

**Goal:** Build confidence, clarity, and a repeatable process. ~30–45 minutes most days; longer on speech days.

**Tools needed:** Phone camera, timer, notebook, optional free Coursera/Saylor enrollment, optional Toastmasters guest visit.

---

## Week 1 — Foundations & nerves

| Day | Focus | Task |
|-----|-------|------|
| 1 | Mindset | Watch Joe Kowan (stage fright) + Chris Anderson (throughline). Write your “why I want to speak better” in 3 sentences. |
| 2 | Throughline | Pick a 2-minute topic. Write one throughline sentence. Cut anything that doesn't support it. |
| 3 | Structure | Outline: Hook → 3 points → Close. Deliver once to camera. |
| 4 | Vocal warm-up | Watch Julian Treasure. Do his vocal exercises. Re-record Day 3 talk with more variety. |
| 5 | PREP drill | Answer 5 random questions with PREP (60 sec each). Record. |
| 6 | Feedback | Watch both recordings. Note: fillers/min, pace, eye contact, clarity of point. |
| 7 | Rest / review | Read one chapter of Anderson or Gallo *or* skim Six Minutes “7 Deadly Sins.” |

**Week 1 checkpoint:** You have a throughline habit and baseline video.

---

## Week 2 — Craft & openings

| Day | Focus | Task |
|-----|-------|------|
| 8 | Openings | Write 3 openings for same talk (story / question / startling fact). Film all three. |
| 9 | Story | Add one personal or customer story using STAR. Keep under 90 seconds. |
| 10 | Sticky ideas | Apply SUCCESs: make one point more concrete + unexpected. |
| 11 | Body language | Practice standing open posture; deliberate gestures on key words. Film waist-up. |
| 12 | Informative talk | Prep 4–5 min informative speech (teach something). Outline only — no full script. |
| 13 | Deliver | Film the informative talk. No restarting mid-take. |
| 14 | Evaluate | Score yourself 1–5 on: structure, opening, vocal variety, gestures, close. |

**Week 2 checkpoint:** Stronger openings; one complete informative talk on video.

---

## Week 3 — Persuasion & live practice

| Day | Focus | Task |
|-----|-------|------|
| 15 | Monroe | Outline a persuasive talk with Monroe’s 5 steps. |
| 16 | Duarte contrast | Mark “what is” vs “what could be” moments in that outline. |
| 17 | Deliver persuasion | Film 5-min persuasive talk. End with clear Action. |
| 18 | Q&A | Have a friend ask 5 tough questions; answer with PREP. |
| 19 | Toastmasters / peer | Visit a Toastmasters club as guest *or* exchange feedback with a peer on videos. |
| 20 | Impromptu | 10× 60-second random-topic talks (MasterTalk-style random word). |
| 21 | Review week | Compare Week 1 and Week 3 videos. List 3 improvements + 1 focus area. |

**Week 3 checkpoint:** Persuasive structure + at least one live or peer feedback loop.

---

## Week 4 — Polish & systems

| Day | Focus | Task |
|-----|-------|------|
| 22 | Signature talk | Build your “signature” 5–7 min talk (career / idea / story). Throughline + outline. |
| 23 | Rehearse | Rehearse aloud 3× standing. Time it. Cut to time. |
| 24 | Full dress | Film full talk once as if live. Optional: run through Yoodli/Orai for filler stats. |
| 25 | Delivery checklist | Use the DOCX delivery checklist. Fix top 2 issues; re-film. |
| 26 | Handling nerves | Pre-talk routine: breathe 4-7-8 ×3, power stance 60s, first line memorized. |
| 27 | Live or simulated | Deliver to a real small audience (friends, club, standup meeting). |
| 28 | Q&A mastery | After talk, take 5 minutes of questions; record answers. |
| 29 | Reflect | Write: What works for me? What’s still hard? Next 30-day focus. |
| 30 | Systematize | Schedule ongoing: 1 filmed practice/week + club or peer feedback 2×/month. Enroll/continue UW Coursera or Saylor if not done. |

**Week 4 checkpoint:** One signature talk delivered live or simulated; a sustainable practice habit.

---

## Daily micro-drills (any day, 5 minutes)

1. **Random word:** Speak 60 seconds on a random object.
2. **One breath pause:** Read a paragraph; insert a full silent pause at each period.
3. **First line:** Memorize and deliver your opening line 5 times with energy.
4. **Filler hunt:** 60-second talk; count um/uh; redo aiming for half as many.

## Suggested resource pairing

- **Days 1–7:** TED craft talks + Toastmasters guest visit planning  
- **Days 8–14:** Gallo or Duarte ideas + Six Minutes articles on openings  
- **Days 15–21:** Monroe + Think Fast Talk Smart podcast episodes on anxiety/Q&A  
- **Days 22–30:** Signature talk + Yoodli/recording feedback
