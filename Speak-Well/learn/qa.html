Lesson 05

Handling Q&A

~7 min read · Q&A

Q&A is where many speakers lose the room — not because they lack knowledge, but because they answer without structure.

Use PREP under pressure

Point → Reason → Example → Point. It buys clarity when adrenaline is high. If you need a beat, say “Great question — here’s the short answer,” then PREP.

Rules that keep you safe

  • Answer the question asked, then stop
  • If you do not know, say so and offer how you will find out
  • Bridge hostile questions back to your throughline without dodging
  • Repeat or rephrase long questions so the whole room hears them

Practice drill

Have a friend ask five tough questions about your talk. Answer each in 60 seconds with PREP. Record. Count fillers. Repeat tomorrow aiming for half as many.

Conversation skills help

Celeste Headlee’s talk 10 ways to have a better conversation is excellent cross-training for listening and responding.
