Lesson 06

Practice systems & feedback

~9 min read · Practice

Reading about speaking does not make you a speaker. Systems do.

Toastmasters

Toastmasters International gives you prepared speeches, Table Topics (impromptu), and structured evaluations. Pathways is their educational track. Visit as a guest before joining.

Weekly recording loop

  1. Film a 2–5 minute talk on your phone
  2. Score yourself 1–5 on structure, opening, vocal variety, gestures, close
  3. Note fillers per minute
  4. Re-film once with one improvement only

AI coaches & tools

  • Yoodli — free tier and paid plans for filler/stats feedback
  • Orai — freemium mobile practice app
  • Built-in phone camera + timer — still the highest ROI tool

Deliberate drills (5 minutes)

  • Random-word 60-second talks
  • One-breath pause at every period
  • First-line delivery × 5
  • Filler hunt: cut um/uh by half on a redo
