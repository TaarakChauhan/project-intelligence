Lesson 03

Delivery: voice, body, openings, closings

~10 min read · Delivery

Delivery is how your structure becomes felt. You do not need theatrical talent — you need controllable variety and a strong first and last 30 seconds.

Vocal variety (Julian Treasure)

Treasure’s toolkit: register, timbre, prosody, pace, pitch, volume, and the strategic pause. Watch: How to speak so that people want to listen.

Practice: read one paragraph three ways — flat, exaggerated, then purposeful. Keep the purposeful version.

Body language

  • Open posture; plant your feet
  • Gesture on key words, then return to rest
  • Eye contact in zones (left / center / right), not a blank stare at notes

Openings that work

Never start with “Today I’m going to talk about…”. Use a story, a question, or a startling fact in the first 15 seconds. Write three openings for the same talk and film all three.

Closings

Close on the throughline. Offer one action, one image, or one memorable line. Then stop — do not trail off into logistics unless needed.

Pre-stage delivery checklist

  • Throughline written as one sentence
  • First line memorized
  • Opening hook tested aloud
  • Keyword outline only (not a full script)
  • Water, timer awareness, tech check
  • Two deliberate pause points marked
  • Clear call to action or takeaway
