Delivery checklist before you walk on stage

Speak Well Blog

  • Throughline written as one sentence
  • First line memorized
  • Opening hook tested aloud
  • Keyword outline only
  • Two pause points marked
  • Water / tech / timer checked
  • Closing line decided
  • Breathing routine done

Print this page or keep it beside your notes. The point is not bureaucracy — it is reducing last-minute chaos.
