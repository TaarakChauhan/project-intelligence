The throughline: one idea that carries everything

Speak Well Blog

Chris Anderson describes a throughline as the connecting theme that makes a talk feel inevitable. Without it, even good stories feel like a playlist.

How to write one

  • Finish this sentence: “If they remember only one thing, it is ___.”
  • Make it specific enough to disagree with.
  • Test every section: “Does this serve the throughline?” If not, cut.
