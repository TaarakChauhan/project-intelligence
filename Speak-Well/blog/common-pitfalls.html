Common public speaking pitfalls — and fixes

Speak Well Blog

PitfallFix
No clear pointWrite a one-sentence throughline; cut everything else
Weak openingHook in 15 seconds: story, question, or startling fact
Slide readingSlides support; speak to people
Filler overloadPause instead of “um”; record and count
Too fastBreathe at paragraph ends; mark pauses
Flat deliveryExaggerate variety, then dial back
Ignoring audienceEye-contact zones; watch faces
Q&A panicUse PREP; buy a beat calmly
Over-relying on notesKeyword outline; rehearse aloud
Avoiding practiceSchedule low-stakes weekly reps
