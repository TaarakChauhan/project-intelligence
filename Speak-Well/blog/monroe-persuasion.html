Persuade with Monroe’s Motivated Sequence

Speak Well Blog

When your talk asks people to do something, Monroe’s Motivated Sequence is a reliable spine:

  1. Attention — hook
  2. Need — make the problem felt
  3. Satisfaction — present the solution
  4. Visualization — help them see the future with/without it
  5. Action — one clear next step

Overview: MindTools. Pair with Duarte’s contrast between “what is” and “what could be.”
