Stop saying um: practice the pause

Speak Well Blog

Fillers are usually fear of silence. The fix is not self-hatred — it is rehearsed pausing.

Filler hunt drill

  1. Speak for 60 seconds on any topic.
  2. Count um/uh/like.
  3. Redo immediately, aiming for half as many.
  4. Replace each filler urge with a full stop and a breath.

Michael Chad Hoeppner’s Don’t Say Um focuses on physical drills that interrupt filler habits — useful if verbal reminders alone are not enough.
