A visual guide to how money really works

Money Atlas is a free visual encyclopedia of money. It explains how money, firms, markets, the state and a household ledger actually work, using original diagrams and plain language instead of jargon. Start with the short history of metal, coinage, the gold standard and fiat money. Then move on to why money exists at all, how a company turns revenue into cash and keeps score with three financial statements, how markets match buyers and sellers, how banks and insurers fit in, how governments tax, borrow and spend, and finally how your own household ledger connects to all of it. Each chapter is a map you can explore at your own pace, with short articles, a glossary and simple tools to test the ideas on real numbers. No sign up is needed and nothing is sold here.
