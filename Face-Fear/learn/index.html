Face Fear · Lesson index

All ten lessons

A path from understanding fear to living with courage. Each lesson has key ideas, a practical exercise, and a reflection prompt.

  1. 01. What fear is for
    Fear is an old survival system. It is not a flaw, and it is not a verdict on you. 8 min read.
  2. 02. Fear in the body and brain
    The amygdala, the prefrontal cortex, adrenaline, cortisol, and the signals your body sends back. 10 min read.
  3. 03. Fight, flight, freeze, and fawn
    The four ways people react under threat, and why none of them is a character flaw. 8 min read.
  4. 04. Fear, anxiety, panic, and phobia
    Four words people use as if they mean the same thing. They do not, and the difference helps. 9 min read.
  5. 05. How fear is learned and unlearned
    Conditioning, extinction, exposure, and why fear sometimes comes back. 11 min read.
  6. 06. The thinking traps
    Catastrophising, threat appraisal, uncertainty, and the avoidance loop that keeps fear alive. 10 min read.
  7. 07. The common fears
    Failure, rejection, judgment, public speaking, change, death, and money. 12 min read.
  8. 08. Fear, risk, and money decisions
    Loss aversion, panic selling, and how to make good decisions when fear is loud. 10 min read.
  9. 09. Fear at work and in founding
    Fear of failure in founders, psychological safety in teams, and leading people who are afraid. 10 min read.
  10. 10. Courage as a practice
    Values, self compassion, sleep, movement, and when to reach out for help. 11 min read.

When you finish, keep the practice toolkit close. It collects every exercise in one printable page.

A note on safety. Face Fear is educational and is not medical advice. If fear or anxiety feels overwhelming, please talk to a doctor or a mental health professional. In Canada, you can call or text 988 any time if you are in crisis. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
