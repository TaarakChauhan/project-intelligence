How to use Face Fear

Fear changes through doing, not only through reading.

1

Understand it

Lessons 01 to 06 explain what fear is, how it is learned, and the thinking habits that keep it going.

2

Apply it

Lessons 07 to 09 look at the fears people share, and at fear in money, work, and leadership.

3

Practise it

Lesson 10 and the toolkit turn it into small, repeatable acts of courage. Print the worksheets and use them weekly.
