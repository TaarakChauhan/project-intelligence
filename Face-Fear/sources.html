Sources and further reading

Face Fear is written in our own words. These are the 43 sources we read to build it, so you can check the evidence and go deeper.

We favoured public health bodies, professional associations, and peer reviewed research. Where the science is still unsettled, such as memory reconsolidation or the fawn response, the lessons say so.

Getting help in Canada

  • Call or text 988 any time for crisis support.
  • Call 911 if you or someone else is in immediate danger.
  • See Government of Canada mental health support and CAMH on anxiety disorders.
