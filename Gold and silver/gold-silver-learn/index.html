Educational Briefing

How did we move from weighing gold and silver to trusting paper and pixels?

For most of recorded economic history, money was a physical good—most often gold or silver. Only in the twentieth century did major economies complete the shift to pure fiat currency.

This briefing is the Metal intro inside Money Atlas — start there if you are walking the studio floor by floor.

A short arc

From Lydia to floating fiat

c. 650–550 BCE Lydia: earliest precious-metal coinage (electrum, then gold)
1870s–1914 Classical international gold standard
1944 Bretton Woods: dollar fixed to gold at $35/oz
1971 Nixon Shock: gold window closed
1973– Floating fiat currencies predominate
