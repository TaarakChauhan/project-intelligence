Survival of the fittest — without the slogan

The phrase is famous, short, and usually wrong in everyday use. This site walks you from the folk meaning to the scientific idea it was meant to name: natural selection.

Start here See the 5 sessions
Keep this definition

Fitness means relative reproductive success in a given environment — not strength, not virtue, not rank on a ladder.

“Survival of the fittest” is an old nickname for natural selection.
