Part E — One-minute self-check

Without looking back, finish the sentence:

"Survival of the fittest, used scientifically, means ________________________________________________, not ________________________________________________."

If you cannot finish it without "strongest," "meanest," or "best people," reread Lesson 1 before you claim you are done.

After you finish

Compare Part C against the five-question checklist at the end of Lesson 2. Then take the quiz if you have not already. The worksheet is passed when Part C names all three ingredients and Part D does not grant the speaker a scientific result they did not earn.
