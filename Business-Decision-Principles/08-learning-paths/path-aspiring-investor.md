# Learning path: aspiring investor

**Purpose:** Build an evidence-led underwriting habit in four weeks.

1. Week 1: Foundations 01–05; start a journal with probabilities.
2. Week 2: VC 01–04; map one real funnel and stage-appropriate risk.
3. Week 3: VC 05–08; complete a diligence checklist and memo.
4. Week 4: Angel 02–05 and Terms 01–04; model a small portfolio and waterfall.

**Deliverable:** One investment memo, one pass memo, and a review of forecast calibration. Discuss with a qualified professional before acting.
