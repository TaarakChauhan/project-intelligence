# Learning path: founder raising

**Purpose:** Prepare for fundraising while keeping the company’s decision quality intact.

1. Start with Foundations 01, 02, and 04.
2. Read VC 01–04 to understand stage evidence and the funnel.
3. Use VC 06–08 to prepare metrics, red flags, and a memo-ready narrative.
4. Read Owner 03–04 and Terms 01–04 before comparing instruments.
5. Complete the founder premortem and cap-table worksheet with counsel.

**Deliverable:** use-of-proceeds plan, downside runway plan, clean evidence room, and questions for each investor.
