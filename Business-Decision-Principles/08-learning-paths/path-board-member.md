# Learning path: board member

**Purpose:** Practise informed oversight without becoming shadow management.

1. Foundations 01, 03, and 05: process, models, and bias safeguards.
2. Boards 01–04: duties, business judgment, boundaries, and conflicts.
3. Terms 02–04: capital, dilution, preferences, and alignment.
4. Practise writing a decision record and conflict protocol for a hypothetical board.

**Deliverable:** a decision-rights schedule and board agenda that surfaces choices, evidence, and conflicts. Seek local counsel for actual duties.

**Sources:** https://corplaw.delaware.gov/delaware-way-business-judgment/ · https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/business_judgment_rule · https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-44/
