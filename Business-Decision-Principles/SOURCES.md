# Curated sources

The lessons synthesise the following public material. Links are provided for further reading; interpretations here are educational and not a substitute for professional advice.

## Decision-making
- Annie Duke, *Thinking in Bets* (author resources): https://www.annieduke.com/thinking-in-bets/
- Amazon shareholder letters, including Jeff Bezos's 1997 letter and one-way/two-way door framing: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazons-original-1997-letter-to-shareholders
- Gary Klein, “Performing a Project Premortem”: https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem
- John Boyd's OODA overview (US Air Force): https://www.airuniversity.af.edu/Portals/10/ASPJ/journals/Chronicles/2019/Vol28_Issue4/Col%20John%20Boyd%20and%20the%20OODA%20Loop.pdf
- Kahneman, Lovallo and Sibony, “Before You Make That Big Decision”: https://hbr.org/2011/06/before-you-make-that-big-decision

## Practical owner material
- Drew Rhoden, decision-making framework for owners: https://www.drewrhoden.com/business-decision-making-a-practical-framework-for-owners-when-every-choice-feels-urgent

**Citation note:** Research figures are quoted only where the linked source supports them. The Gompers et al. survey covered 885 VCs at 681 firms; approximately 95% mentioned team as important and approximately 47% called it the most important factor; business model was mentioned by approximately 83%, product by approximately 74%, and market by approximately 68%. Deal selection ranked most important among sourcing, selection, and value-add activities.
