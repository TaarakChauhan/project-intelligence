# Investment memo outline

1. **Decision requested:** amount, instrument, ownership, conditions, reserve.
2. **One-paragraph thesis:** why this company, market, team, and timing.
3. **Evidence:** verified facts vs management claims vs assumptions.
4. **Market and customer:** ICP, pain, timing, TAM/SAM/SOM method, competition.
5. **Product and technology:** behaviour, retention, differentiation, risks.
6. **Team:** founder-market fit, roles, references, hiring gaps.
7. **Economics:** revenue quality, cohorts, margins, CAC/payback, burn/runway.
8. **Legal/ESG:** entity, IP, contracts, privacy, regulatory and reputation risks.
9. **Scenario table:** base, upside, downside; key sensitivities.
10. **Terms and portfolio fit:** ownership, governance, reserves, conflicts.
11. **Risks and mitigants:** three material risks, evidence, owner, trigger.
12. **Recommendation:** approve, pass, or defer with dated evidence request.
13. **Dissent / open items:** what remains unresolved.

**Educational note:** Outline only; not investment, legal, or accounting advice.
