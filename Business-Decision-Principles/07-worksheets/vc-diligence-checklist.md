# VC diligence checklist

**Use as a prompt, not as a substitute for specialist diligence.**

## Thesis and mandate
- [ ] Why this company, why now, why this fund?
- [ ] Stage, ownership, cheque, reserves, and portfolio fit are explicit.
- [ ] Decision criteria and pass/defer triggers are pre-committed.

## Commercial and product
- [ ] ICP, buyer, workflow, alternatives, pricing, and competition tested.
- [ ] TAM/SAM/SOM method is transparent.
- [ ] Cohort retention, activation, expansion, and churn reconcile to source data.
- [ ] Product/tech architecture, security, IP, and roadmap reviewed as material.

## Team and references
- [ ] Roles, founder-market fit, hiring gaps, and founder conflict tested.
- [ ] At least one independently sourced reference.
- [ ] Bad-news and disagreement behaviour observed.

## Financial, legal, ESG
- [ ] Cash, burn, runway, liabilities, cap table, and forecast reconciled.
- [ ] Incorporation, IP assignment, employment, contracts, privacy, and regulatory items scoped.
- [ ] Material ESG, safety, reputation, or concentration risks recorded.

## Decision
- [ ] Facts, claims, assumptions, and open items separated.
- [ ] Base/upside/downside and key sensitivities shown.
- [ ] Memo recommends approve, pass, or dated defer.

**Educational note:** Checklist only; obtain professional advice where appropriate.
