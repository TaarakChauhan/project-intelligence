# Deal flow to investment committee

**Purpose:** Map a disciplined VC funnel and make stage gates explicit.

**Who this is for:** Investors, founders preparing for diligence, and operators joining an IC.

## Core principles
- **Screen:** fit with mandate, founder contact, problem, and initial evidence.
- **Deep dive:** test the thesis through customers, product, market, team, economics, and risks.
- **Partner review:** expose the case to a partner who can sponsor it and a partner who can challenge it.
- **Memo:** state facts, assumptions, valuation/terms, risks, mitigants, and the actual decision requested.
- **IC:** decide approve, pass, defer with a dated evidence request, or change terms.

## How decisions actually get made
Set a written gate for each step. Avoid “diligence theatre”: every requested item should answer a material question. Keep a pass log so sourcing teams learn which patterns matter. Before IC, circulate the memo early enough for independent views; record conflicts and abstentions.

## Worked mini-example
At screen, a SaaS company fits sector and stage. Deep dive reveals reported growth includes a one-off annual contract. The memo normalises the metric, shows a lower base case, and asks whether retention supports a repeatable motion. IC approves only a smaller amount with a milestone-based reserve plan—or passes.

## Common mistakes
- Moving a deal forward because time has already been spent.
- Making the memo a sales document rather than a falsifiable thesis.
- Treating “more data” as the same as “better evidence.”
- Leaving decision rights and follow-on authority unclear.

## Practice exercise (10 minutes)
Create five gates for a hypothetical deal. For each, write the question, evidence required, owner, and stop/defer rule.
