# Start Here: a 30-minute orientation

**Purpose:** Build a repeatable decision habit before learning specialist investor, owner, or board techniques.

## The first 30 minutes
1. **Minutes 0–5 — Frame it.** Write the decision, decision owner, deadline, objective, constraints, and the do-nothing option. Label it a one-way or two-way door.
2. **Minutes 5–10 — Make uncertainty visible.** List three assumptions, a base/upside/downside case, and what evidence would change your mind.
3. **Minutes 10–15 — Stress-test.** Use inversion (“How could this fail?”), a pre-mortem, and second-order effects.
4. **Minutes 15–20 — Choose a process.** Decide what must be researched, who has a voice, who decides, and what trigger would cause a review.
5. **Minutes 20–25 — Record it.** Use [decision-journal-template.md](07-worksheets/decision-journal-template.md); assign rough probabilities rather than hiding behind certainty.
6. **Minutes 25–30 — Schedule learning.** Put a review date in the calendar and define leading indicators.

## A simple operating loop
**Frame → options → evidence → decision rights → commit → observe → review.** The loop is deliberately lightweight: use more ceremony only when stakes, irreversibility, or uncertainty justify it.
