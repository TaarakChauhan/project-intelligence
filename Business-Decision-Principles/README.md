# Business Decision Principles
> **Start with the full guide:** [MASTER-DOCUMENT.md](MASTER-DOCUMENT.md) is the complete consolidated synthesis of this library.


A practical, original learning library for making better decisions as an investor, VC, angel, founder, owner, or board member. It focuses on process: framing the choice, separating controllable process from luck, gathering proportionate evidence, assigning decision rights, and learning afterwards.

## Who it is for
- **Investors and VCs:** underwriting uncertainty, diligence, portfolio construction, and investment memos.
- **Angels:** fast, ethical checks on team, market, model integrity, terms, and fit with a portfolio.
- **Founders and owners:** hiring, pricing, building, buying, pivoting, financing, and cash survival.
- **Board members:** oversight, fiduciary duties, conflicts, and the boundary between governance and management.

## How to learn
Start with [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md), then keep a decision journal. Read one lesson, complete its 5–15 minute exercise, and revisit the journal after the result is known. Use the worksheets as working documents; duplicate them for each live decision.

## Working rule
A good decision can produce a bad result; a bad process can get lucky. Record the information, assumptions, alternatives, and odds you actually had at the time.
