# Owner decision operating system

**Purpose:** Make recurring operating decisions visible, owned, and reviewable.

**Who this is for:** Business owners, founders, and functional leaders.

## Core principles
- Classify the decision by stakes, reversibility, urgency, and uncertainty.
- Name one decision owner, contributors, approver, deadline, and review date.
- Include alternatives, including do nothing or wait for a signal.
- Define leading indicators and a kill/adjust trigger.
- Review process and outcome without blame.

## How decisions actually get made
Maintain a weekly decision queue. For each item, write the one-sentence question, decision class, evidence needed, and next action. Use a short memo for one-way doors; use experiments for two-way doors. Escalate only decisions that truly exceed delegated authority.

## Worked mini-example
A 12-person firm must choose a CRM. The owner names the sales lead as decision owner, tests two tools with a small team, defines adoption and reporting criteria, and schedules a 30-day review. The owner retains budget approval without micromanaging configuration.

## Common mistakes
- Confusing urgency with importance.
- Having many advisers but no owner.
- Starting research without a decision deadline.
- Never closing the loop after implementation.
