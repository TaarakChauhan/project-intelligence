# Light diligence playbook

**Purpose:** Complete a focused, ethical first diligence pass without pretending it is a full audit.

**Who this is for:** Angels with limited time and founders preparing a clean data room.

## Core principles
- Ask only for evidence tied to a material decision.
- Verify identity, incorporation, ownership, IP assignment, material contracts, liabilities, customer claims, and financing history as appropriate.
- Source at least one reference independently and ask for a churned or non-buying perspective where possible.
- Protect confidential data; do not circulate customer information unnecessarily.

## How decisions actually get made
Use a 48-hour screen, then a one-week deepened pass if the thesis survives. Reconcile the cap table and instrument terms. Flag legal, privacy, employment, regulatory, and related-party issues for qualified advisers rather than offering legal conclusions.

## Worked mini-example
The pitch says “no debt.” A bank statement and liabilities schedule reveal a revenue-based financing obligation. The issue may be manageable, but it changes cash runway, priority, and the next-round story.

## Common mistakes
- Asking for sensitive data before establishing trust.
- Treating “standard terms” as automatically safe.
- Relying on a founder-selected customer list.
- Calling a checklist complete when material items remain unknown.

## Practice exercise (15 minutes)
Build a red/amber/green table for ownership, cash, customers, IP, team, terms, and compliance. Put a specific next action beside every red or amber item.
