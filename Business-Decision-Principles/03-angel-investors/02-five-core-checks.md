# Five core checks for an angel

**Purpose:** Run a fast, evidence-led first pass before deeper work.

**Who this is for:** Angels evaluating an early-stage company.

## Core principles
1. **Founder-market fit:** Why this team, for this problem, now?
2. **Market timing and wedge:** Who buys first, what changed, and how can the beachhead expand?
3. **Model integrity:** Do metrics reconcile to customers, contracts, cash, and cohorts?
4. **Momentum:** Is learning or adoption accelerating, and is it repeatable rather than a one-off?
5. **Terms and fit:** Do the SAFE/note/round terms, ownership, information, and follow-on expectations fit your plan?

## How decisions actually get made
Start with a 30-minute founder conversation and a small document request. Verify one customer or user behaviour, one financial/metric claim, one reference you source independently, and the instrument terms. Move to deeper diligence only if the answers increase confidence.

## Worked mini-example
A company shows 50 pilot logos. The five checks reveal most are unpaid and inactive, while two paid customers expand usage. The real thesis becomes narrower but potentially stronger: prove repeatability in that segment before scaling the headline logo count.

## Common mistakes
- Counting logos instead of retained behaviour.
- Calling momentum “viral” without a cohort or referral definition.
- Ignoring conversion mechanics because the cheque is small.
- Asking only founder-provided references.

## Practice exercise (12 minutes)
Score each check 0 (unknown), 1 (asserted), 2 (partly evidenced), or 3 (independently evidenced). Write the one question that could move the total most.
