# Founder-market fit

**Purpose:** Assess whether founders have an unusual, testable advantage in understanding and serving a market.

**Who this is for:** Angels, VCs, and founders articulating their edge.

## Core principles
- Look for earned insight, customer access, relevant craft, and speed of learning—not only résumés.
- Founder-market fit can be acquired through sustained immersion, but claims need evidence.
- A mission matters when it supports persistence and customer empathy; it does not override model weakness.
- Complementarity and conflict-handling are team evidence.

## How decisions actually get made
Ask founders to describe a customer’s workflow, failed experiments, non-obvious constraint, and the next falsifiable test. Speak with customers or domain experts independently. Explore how the team handles disagreement and bad news.

## Worked mini-example
A founder without a traditional industry title has spent three years serving the target users, can name the workflow bottleneck, and has built a distribution partnership. That may be stronger fit than a famous employer with no customer access.

## Common mistakes
- Equating prestige with fit.
- Romanticising founder obsession.
- Ignoring co-founder role gaps and unresolved conflict.
- Treating a personal story as market proof.

## Practice exercise (10 minutes)
Write the team’s five strongest “earned advantages.” For each, name the evidence, the competitor who could copy it, and the next way to deepen it.
