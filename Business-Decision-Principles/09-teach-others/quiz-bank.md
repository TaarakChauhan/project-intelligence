# Quiz bank

1. **Q:** Does a good outcome prove a good decision? **A:** No; separate process from luck.
2. **Q:** What is a two-way door? **A:** A decision reversible at tolerable cost.
3. **Q:** Name three premortem questions. **A:** What failed, why, and what can we prevent or monitor?
4. **Q:** What must a VC memo distinguish? **A:** Facts, claims, assumptions, and open questions.
5. **Q:** What stage shift occurs from seed to Series A? **A:** More evidence of repeatable demand, retention, and growth economics.
6. **Q:** Why can blended LTV/CAC mislead? **A:** Cohorts, margins, churn, and acquisition channels differ.
7. **Q:** Name five angel checks. **A:** Founder-market fit, timing/wedge, model integrity, momentum, and terms/fit.
8. **Q:** Is a SAFE ordinary equity today? **A:** No; it is a contract for future equity subject to its terms.
9. **Q:** What is the board/management boundary? **A:** Board oversight and reserved decisions; management execution within delegated authority.
10. **Q:** Does the business judgment rule protect every board decision? **A:** No; jurisdiction, process, good faith, authority, and conflicts matter.
11. **Q:** What should a cash runway trigger do? **A:** Convert a defined risk threshold into a pre-agreed action.
12. **Q:** Why record a pass? **A:** To learn which evidence and mandate constraints matter.
