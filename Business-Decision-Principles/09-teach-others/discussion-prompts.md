# Discussion prompts

1. When is a bad outcome evidence of a bad process, and when is it noise?
2. What makes a decision truly irreversible in your business?
3. Which assumption in your current plan is most fragile?
4. What would a customer, churned customer, or sceptical reference say?
5. When does a large TAM become a distraction?
6. Which metric creates the most dangerous incentive?
7. What does your board need to know versus decide?
8. How should a conflicted director participate, if at all, under the applicable process?
9. Which term sheet term changes behaviour in a downside scenario?
10. What would make you pass on a good company or opportunity?

**Facilitation rule:** Invite a dissenting view, distinguish facts from claims, and end with a test or decision owner.
