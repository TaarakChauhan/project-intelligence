# 90-minute workshop: better business decisions

**Audience:** mixed group of owners, investors, founders, or board learners.

- 0–10: Introduce outcome bias; share one lucky win and one process miss.
- 10–25: Frame a live decision using the journal template.
- 25–40: Classify one-way/two-way doors; design a reversible test.
- 40–55: Teams apply EV, inversion, and a premortem.
- 55–70: Investor lens: stage evidence, diligence, red flags, and memo.
- 70–82: Governance lens: decision rights, conflicts, and escalation.
- 82–90: Individual commitment and review date.

**Facilitator notes:** Ask for evidence, not personal verdicts. Keep legal/financial questions in the “consult a professional” lane. Use the quiz bank for a five-minute close.

**Sources:** https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem · https://www.nber.org/papers/w22587
